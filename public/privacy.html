Privacy policy

Effective 4 September 2026

Sparky is operated by Erlin Hoxha. This policy explains how the Sparky iPhone/iPad app and electrics.pages.dev handle your information.

Study without an account

You can use the app's study materials and tools without signing in. Preferences, saved notes, training results and calculation history stay on your device. Exam progress is also stored locally, and is synchronised to your account if you sign in. Device and operating-system backups may include local app data under your Apple settings.

Optional account information

For an account we store your email, an account identifier, a securely hashed password, and any nickname or profile image you choose to provide. Signed-in exam progress includes answers, test selection, submission status and update times so you can resume on another device. Your password is transmitted over HTTPS for authentication and is not saved by the native app. A session token is stored in the iOS Keychain.

How we use and share information

We use account information to provide sign-in, profile editing and progress synchronisation. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. There are no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the native app.

Cloudflare provides website hosting, API processing and account database storage. Requests necessarily include network information such as your IP address. IP addresses and account emails are used in temporary abuse-prevention counters. Cloudflare may process security and operational information under its service policies, including outside your country. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Account and synchronised progress information remains while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time. After you confirm your password and deletion, the account, profile image and cloud exam progress are removed from the active database and existing sessions stop working. Limited backup and security records may remain for the hosting provider's retention period; they are not used to restore your account for ordinary use.

Deleting your account does not erase offline study data from your other devices. Use Settings → Reset study and training progress and Reset all exam attempts, or remove the app, to clear local study data. Abuse-prevention counters are periodically pruned after their retention window.

Your choices and rights

You can study without an account, sign out, edit your profile, clear local progress or delete your account. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your personal data, or object to or restrict its use. Account services process data to provide the service you request; security processing supports our legitimate interest in protecting it. You may raise a concern with your local data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact erlin.hx@gmail.com. Please do not send your password.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change.

Sparky support

Help with electrical revision, site tools and your Sparky account.

Contact support

Email erlin.hx@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model and what happened. For a question correction, include the subject, test and question number. Never send passwords or other people's personal information.

Do I need an account?

No. Study materials, local progress and tools work without signing in. An existing Sparky account enables profile editing and exam-progress synchronisation with the web app.

My progress is not synchronising

Check your internet connection and that you are using the same account on each device. Open your profile to check the sync status. Offline answers stay on your device until the next successful sync.

Manage my data

You can reset local study data in Settings. To permanently remove your account and cloud progress, use Delete account. Read the privacy policy for details.

Using electrical study material

Sparky is a study and calculation aid. Apply the current official regulations, guidance, site conditions and competent professional judgement when carrying out electrical work. Practice results do not confer a qualification or authorise electrical work.
